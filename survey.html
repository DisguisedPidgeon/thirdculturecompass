Early, informal survey

Tell us what it was really like

Takes about 5 minutes. Your answers help us understand what moving between school systems is like for students. This is an early, informal student project.

Why this matters

Not much has been written from the student's side about moving between school systems. With your answers, we may share anonymized summaries on this site to help future students. This is not a formal study, and every response helps.

Before you start
  • This is an early, informal project run by a high school student, not a school, university, or research institution. It's meant for people 13 and older. If you're under 18, please check with a parent or guardian first.
  • It takes about 5 minutes. Only questions 1 and 3 are required. Skip anything you'd rather not answer.
  • Please leave out names of people and schools in your written answers. Written answers can sometimes identify someone even without a name.
  • Your answers are anonymous unless you share your email in question 12. If you do, it's used only to tell you when results are shared or to ask whether you'd like to be featured. It won't be sold or shared.
  • Anonymized summaries and short quotes may be shared on this site, or with educators and researchers who give feedback. Raw responses and emails are never shared.
  • Answers are collected through Formspree, a form service, and seen only by the people running this project. This site also uses Google Analytics to count visits.
  • To ask a question or have your response removed, use the contact form.

By submitting, you agree to take part on these terms.
